个人简介 Profile

我是斯图加特大学与雅典大学哲学系的联合博士候选人，研究黑格尔哲学， 尤其关注其逻辑学与实在哲学（自然哲学）之间的关系，并尝试结合当代科学 基础概念（如"力""场""概率"）来更新黑格尔式哲学。

此前，我在牛津大学获得哲学硕士学位（BPhil → MSt）， 在北京大学获得哲学学士学位。

我也对胡塞尔现象学、人工智能哲学、语言哲学与传统中国哲学抱有浓厚兴趣， 欢迎一切相关学术交流。

I am a joint (cotutelle) PhD candidate in philosophy at the University of Stuttgart and the University of Athens. My research focuses on Hegel's philosophy, particularly the relation between his Logik and Realphilosophie (especially Naturphilosophie), and attempts to renew Hegelian philosophy through introducing foundational concepts of contemporary science (e.g. "force", "field", "probability", etc.).

Previously, I received an MSt in Philosophy from the University of Oxford and a BA in Philosophy from Peking University.

I am also deeply interested in Husserlian phenomenology, philosophy of AI, philosophy of language, and traditional Chinese philosophy. All academic inquiries are warmly welcome.

博士研究 Doctoral Research

Research Proposal

讨论黑格尔的逻辑学、实在哲学尤其是自然哲学的关系，试图结合当代科学基础概念 （如"力""场""概率"）来更新黑格尔式哲学。

My doctoral research is proposed to study the relation between Hegel's Logik and Realphilosophie (esp. Naturphilosophie). I attempt to combine cutting-edge foundational scientific concepts (like "force", "field", "probability") to renew the philosophy under Hegel's spirit.
